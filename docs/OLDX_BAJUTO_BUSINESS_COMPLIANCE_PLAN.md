## 3. Tax and special-category gate
- Free listings and direct off-platform payments are intended to reduce complexity, not an automatic GST exemption. Assess CGST Act sections 2(44)/(45), 9(5), 22, 24 and 52 and current notifications for exact actual service flows. Normal services threshold in Gujarat generally INR 20 lakh aggregate turnover, subject to statutory exceptions. Do not apply threshold mechanically to e-commerce operator/TCS or notified services. No paid subscriptions/ads, commission, platform-mediated payment, booking or checkout until relevant registration, invoice/GST/TDS/income-tax/accounting requirements are reviewed. Record revenue and expenses from commencement as applicable.
- Avoid restricted/illegal items. For food, medicine, financial services, real estate and other regulated listings, verify category-specific licensing and seller/platform obligations before enabling. Directory-only vs facilitating actual orders/bookings matters. Block or constrain unsupported categories by policy and enforcement. No wallet, escrow or third-party settlement without assessing RBI/payment requirements; no unauthorized activities.
